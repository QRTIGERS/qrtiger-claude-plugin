# QR Tiger for Claude

QR Tiger lists, creates, and manages QR codes, folders, and scan statistics for a connected account. Connect the QR Tiger connector in Claude and sign in with a Professional or Enterprise account. A free plan is refused at consent and cannot use the connector. After you sign in, QR data is sent only to the signed-in QR Tiger account through api.qrtiger.com. The plugin stores nothing itself. Archive, edit, and delete wait for confirmation in the chat before any change is made.
